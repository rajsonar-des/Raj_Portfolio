Spatial ARfor ATC Towers

A spatial AR interface for aerodrome controllers that places flight data, weather and traffic right where they look: out of the tower window.

Field research at ATC Tower, Kempegowda International Airport, Bengaluru, 7 Nov 2026

Eyes on the runway, data on the screens.

The problem

Controllers must link digital information (flight data, weather, traffic) with what they see outside the tower window. That means constant glances between screens and runway, which costs attention and situational awareness.

The brief

Replace multiple screens with one spatial UI in AR that tags what’s happening outside the window, overlaying data directly on the runway, apron and taxiways.

Hypotheses

Better situational awareness, less attention switching, and better anticipation and coordination.

Data where the aircraft is.

A spatial AR interface that shows selected operational information in context, aligned with the aircraft and runway controllers see through the window, while approved systems and direct observation stay in place.

  • Shows aircraft identity, status and surface movement, placed on the aircraft itself.
  • Supports, not replaces: approved displays remain the authority for operational data.
  • Protects attention by cutting the switching between screens and the outside view.
